# Limitations And Roadmap

## Current Limitations

- Local/demo mode only.
- Synthetic data only.
- No Bloomberg, Refinitiv, ICE, or CME data redistribution.
- No authentication.
- No database persistence.
- No deployment packaging.
- No trade execution.
- Hedge outputs require human review.
- Not financial advice.
- Not a CTRM replacement yet.

## Roadmap

- Deployment packaging.
- Authentication and user management.
- Database persistence for runs, portfolios, and audit history.
- Richer commodity and product coverage.
- External market data connector architecture using bring-your-own-data / bring-your-own-license.
- Portfolio and book management.
- Richer reporting packs and reviewer views.
- Production-grade audit trail.
- Wider scenario libraries and historical analytics.
